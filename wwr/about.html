Our Mission

Here at White Water Rafting Co., our whitewater rafting trips are designed to provide thrill-seekers with an unforgettable experience on the water. Our expert guides ensure safety while you navigate through stunning landscapes and challenging rapids. Oars Up!

History

White Water Rafting Co. was founded in 1998 by a group of adventure enthusiasts who wanted to share their love for the great outdoors. Starting with just a handful of rafts and a passion for exploration, the company has grown into a leading provider of whitewater rafting experiences.

Over the decades we have guided thousands of families, students, and thrill-seekers through some of the most breathtaking river corridors in the world. Our commitment to safety, environmental stewardship, and unforgettable memories has never wavered since day one.
